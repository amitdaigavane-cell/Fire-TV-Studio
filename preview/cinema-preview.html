Layout

Weather top left, HOME bottom centre

The weather card takes the HOME spot; HOME moves to the middle of the bottom bar. Your Home name from the phone app still works and keeps full size up to 24 characters (today it shrinks past 12).

Dock

Five apps and More apps always visible

The dock shows five favourites plus More apps. Pin more than five and the row slides as you move right, with dots showing where you are; More apps stays pinned at the end.

Glass

Clear glass

Almost no tint or blur, so the wallpaper shows through; a bright edge and inner highlights give the glass shape. Code: CinemaDock

Wallpaper

Video wallpapers

Try one with "Use my photo or video". On the TV this needs a short, muted 1080p H.264 loop; it pauses when an app opens.

Idle

Weather card and live weather

The same card sits top right. The idle screen shows snow, blizzard, rain, storm, fog, cloud, a sun glare with soft light rays by day, and moonlight with stars and a shooting star every 60 seconds by night. Effects tone down on bright wallpapers. Pick "Outside" above to try each; values are a sample.
